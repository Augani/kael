# Third-party assets

`kael_ui` includes the following third-party assets. Their licenses are kept
beside the files they cover so both source checkouts and crate archives carry
the required notices.

| Asset | Version or snapshot | License | Notice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inter | 4.001 (`9221beed3`) | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | [`assets/fonts/LICENSE-INTER`](assets/fonts/LICENSE-INTER) |
| JetBrains Mono | 2.304 | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | [`assets/fonts/LICENSE-JETBRAINS-MONO`](assets/fonts/LICENSE-JETBRAINS-MONO) |
| Lucide icons | 0.545.0 | ISC, with MIT-covered Feather-derived portions | [`assets/icons/LICENSE-LUCIDE`](assets/icons/LICENSE-LUCIDE) |

The Inter and JetBrains Mono font files are included in the published crate.
The Lucide SVG catalog is repository-only and is intentionally excluded from
crate archives; its notice remains beside the catalog for repository users.
